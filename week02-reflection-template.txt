## Plan vs. Implementation

5. Which part of the work took more or less time than expected? Why?

Answer: Setting up the Authors collection and connecting books to their respective authors took more time than expected. I had to ensure that the author references worked correctly and that the database relationships were properly handled. Testing and fixing issues also took some extra time, but I learned a lot from the process.




6. How did Swagger help you in your work this week?

Answer: Swagger made it easier to document and test my API endpoints without having to use a separate API testing tool. I could test GET, POST, PUT, and DELETE requests, check responses, and identify errors. It also helped me confirm that my API was working correctly both locally and after deployment on Render.

7. What is one specific habit or process you will apply next week?

Answer: Next week, I will make it a habit to test each endpoint immediately after implementing it before moving to the next task.
